About

Behind the lens, with care.

Sha Cinematic Studio is a photography and videography studio working across Kerala and Karnataka, covering weddings, events and inaugurations. Every frame is planned for the light, the feeling and the people in it, so the album feels like your day, not a template.

From the first bridal portrait to the last dance, you get natural expressions, soft cinematic colour and a calm photographer who makes the whole day easier.
